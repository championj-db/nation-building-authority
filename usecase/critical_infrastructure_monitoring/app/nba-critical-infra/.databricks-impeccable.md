## Design Context

### Surface type
Databricks App (AppKit: Node + React), single-screen operations console with a review dialog.

### Audience
Customer-facing field demo for public-sector regulators (heavy-vehicle / critical infrastructure
compliance officers and their executives). Shown on laptops and projectors.

### Theme
Light only. Forced regardless of OS preference (`color-scheme: light`).

### Use case
A compliance officer prioritises risk alerts, reviews the contributing evidence for an identified
vehicle and operator, and records a human-in-the-loop decision to route or keep monitoring.

### Brand (locked)
- Palette: Lava 600 #FF3621 (10% accent), Navy 900 #0B2026, Light #F9F7F4
- Typography: DM Sans 400/500/700 + DM Mono
- Components: Du Bois patterns (custom components match Du Bois radius, density, focus ring, states)
- Voice: Databricks editorial-plain (no superlatives, plain statements)

### Additional constraints
- Layout, copy and interactions follow design/critical-infrastructure-operations.html; only the
  visual identity is re-skinned to the Databricks brand.
- Severity must never rely on Lava alone: Lava is the brand accent *and* the error colour, so
  critical risk always pairs colour with a text label.
- Fonts are self-hosted (no CDN dependency at demo time).
- WCAG 2.2 AA.
